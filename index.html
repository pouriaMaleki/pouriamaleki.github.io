About me

I'm a software engineer with more than 11 years of experience in software development, team leadership, and management. Currently at Relex Solutions, where I work as a lead engineer.

JavaScript is my home turf. I know the language well, and I know the culture around it too. I've since moved into a lead engineer role, which suits me well because there's a lot more to software than writing code. I enjoy product design, shaping ways of working, and building strong teams. I'm not deeply fluent in every language, but I enjoy finding my way around most of them. AI tools have opened up even more possibilities, and I've been using them effectively across many different codebases.

Yes, I did put AI tools on my page. I'm glad we have the technology, and I love having it in my toolbox. It brings back the same kind of excitement I felt when I first learned to code as a teenager, and since then I've been building all kinds of things, some serious and some just for the fun of it.
